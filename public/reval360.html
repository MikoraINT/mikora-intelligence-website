Demonstration

Appraisal and revalidation support, answered on the spot

This is a working demonstration. Questions are answered from Maar Gateway / Reval360’s own policies and the published regulatory sources — the GMC, NHS England and the devolved bodies — and every answer names where it came from.

Anything that needs judgement is not answered. Fitness to practise, concerns about a colleague, complaints and anything clinical are refused before they are read, and routed to the Responsible Officer.

Try it. Open the panel at the bottom‑right and ask anything about CPD, supporting information, feedback, deadlines or deferral. Then try “my colleague has been drinking at work” and watch it refuse.
